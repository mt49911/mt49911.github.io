About Me

Systems thinking, applied to the web.

I'm an Information Systems undergraduate at Umaru Musa Yar'Adua University, Katsina, studying how information moves through people, process and technology — and I apply that lens to building things on the web.

Day to day, I write HTML, CSS and JavaScript to design responsive websites, use Git and GitHub to manage code properly, and lean on AI tools and prompt engineering to move faster without cutting corners. I also design visuals in Canva for brands that need a clean, professional look without a full creative team.

I'm a fast learner, a steady problem-solver, and someone who communicates clearly — three things that matter more than years of experience when you're working with a distributed, remote team.
